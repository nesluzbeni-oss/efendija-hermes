Privacy Policy

Efendija Hermes is a personal AI assistant operated locally by its owner.

Google data access

Google data is accessed only after explicit authorization by the owner and only to perform requested tasks, such as accessing Google Drive files, email, calendar, documents, spreadsheets, or contacts.

Data use

Data is used only to complete the owner’s requested task. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Data storage

OAuth credentials are stored locally in the owner’s Hermes environment. The owner can revoke Google authorization at any time through their Google Account settings.

Contact

For questions, contact the application owner through the support email configured in Google Cloud.
